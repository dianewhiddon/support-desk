# Support Desk

## Product

A self-hosted client-support application for freelancers and small studios. Clients submit requests, files, voice notes and links; an owner manages one queue and conversation per request. Success is an understandable request, a traceable response, and a clear next state.

## Retained behavior

Email-link sign-in, active-client allowlist, owner queue, private notes and drafts, guarded downloads, revisions, idempotent sends, email delivery status, optional on-demand AI, optional trusted-agent API. No production data, live integrations, hosted executor or deployment of Diane's own desk.

## Visual commitment

Extend the existing task-first interface with the approved public-app family: black, white and gray, gold actions (#AD8326), lighter gold (#E8DBBE) for accents. Readable ink labels on gold. System typography, restrained borders, 3px corners, light and dark themes. Preserve working layouts and information order. No new marketing identity or decorative animation.

## Audience and access

The owner operates the queue throughout a workday on desktop or phone. Clients use it intermittently, often on phones. Clear labels, visible focus, keyboard tabs, 44px targets and useful errors are required. Every admin and agent token is trusted across all clients; this is a single-business app.

## Release boundary

MIT public source distribution. Users deploy their own copy. Public preparation happens in a separate repository; the live Portal and Rabbit Hole source are preserved.
